Central Thesis

The Big Idea

Vitamin D signaling is adaptively beneficial, not inherently beneficial. The health benefits of sun exposure actually derive from cutaneous inflammation (driving toxin excretion through skin) and blood irradiation (making red blood cells fitter and strengthening iron containment)—two mechanisms that work independently of vitamin D synthesis.

Sunshine vs. Supplementation

A fresh look at the relationship between sunlight, vitamin D, and health

Sunlight ≠ Vitamin D

They are separate entities. Health benefits attributed to vitamin D are largely a byproduct of sunlight's direct elemental effects on the body.

The VitD–Iron Axis

Iron, not calcium, is proposed as the true biological "target" of the vitamin D system

Proposed
Fe

Iron (True Target?)

● VitD suppresses hepcidin, directly increasing iron absorption

● Proximal small bowel enterocytes (iron zone) are most vitD-responsive

● Body needs only ~0.06 moles Fe — active transport makes sense

● ~30% of iron-deprivation genes are vitD-regulated

● VitD regulates transferrin & transferrin receptor expression

● Iron chelators upregulate VDR synthesis

💡 The vitD system appears enlivened whenever iron is scarce.

Conventional
Ca

Calcium (Not the Target?)

● Body needs ~400x more Ca than Fe — active transport too risky at scale

● Ca is absorbed passively in distal bowel, even without vitD

● No limit to passive Ca absorption — unlimited Ca without vitD

● Active transport imports toxic elements alongside Ca

● Oral Ca supplements work fine without vitD

● VitD's calcemic effects come partly from bone resorption, not just absorption

VitD is proposed to be inessential for Ca absorption. The reality is more complex than commonly taught.

Iron Withholding Defense System

Historically, humans lost iron through blood loss (parasites, injuries). Modern life provides unprecedented protection against iron loss, leaving most people in unnatural iron repletion. PFKE via sun exposure strengthens the body's ability to contain this excess iron, countering an evolutionary mismatch.

Sections 5–13

Concerns About Oral Vitamin D

The treatise raises questions about the safety of oral supplementation

1

Bypasses bioregulatory steps

▶
Oral vitD enters the blood through the absorptive epithelium, bypassing critical bioregulatory steps that endogenous vitD must pass. This can deregulate dietary mineral absorption, and since mineral transporters are indiscriminate, toxic elements get absorbed alongside essential ones.
2

Enhances toxic element absorption

▶
Oral vitD has been shown to enhance absorption of every environmental toxin tested (except mercury): Aluminum (Al), Cadmium (Cd), Cobalt (Co), and Lead (Pb). This is essentially the opposite of what LITME achieves through skin excretion.
3

Creates an ironic feedback loop

▶
The toxins brought in by oral vitD interfere with vitD metabolism itself, causing end-organ resistance. Therapeutic effects fade, vitD levels drop, prompting more supplementation—a self-reinforcing cycle where oral vitD antagonizes its own biological activity.
4

Increases iron burden without increasing excretion

▶
Oral vitD boosts iron absorption without boosting iron excretion, elevating total iron burden. Since excess iron is linked to vascular disease, malignancies, and accelerated aging, this supplementation may act as a chronic stimulus for iron accumulation.

Looking Forward

Proposed Implications

If validated, these mechanisms could reshape preventive medicine

🧠

Neurological Health

Regular heliotherapy could reduce neurodegenerative diseases, autism, and cognitive decline by reducing neurotoxic metal accumulation.

❤️

Cardiovascular

Managing iron stores and reducing toxic metal burden may address root causes of vascular disease, not just correlate with vitD levels.

⏱

Aging & Longevity

Excess iron and toxic metals are positioned as central aging drivers. PFKE and LITME directly counteract these processes.

🏦

Clinical Practice

The author proposes oral vitD should eventually be prescription-only, administered via transdermal, IV, or subcutaneous routes.

🌱

Public Health

A return to heliotherapy plus retirement of oral vitD could lower all-cause morbidity and mortality to new record lows.

🔬

Research Needed

Longitudinal studies comparing sun-only cohorts against supplement users to differentiate sunlight effects from vitamin D.
